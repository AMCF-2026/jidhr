**What the client does** — `clients/csuite.py:588` `get_event_date()` is a bare `self._request("event/display/eventdate", ...)`. `_request` (`clients/csuite.py:63`) returns `{success, data, messages}` with CSuite's `data` passed through untouched. **The client does not flatten or rename anything.**

About Us

American Muslim Community Foundation (AMCF) is a national community foundation that advances charitable giving through Donor-Advised Funds, Giving Circles, endowments, and fiscal sponsorships. Since 2017, AMCF has distributed over $28 million to 1000+ nonprofits, supporting basic social needs, racial justice, and health equity.

Charitable giving (zakat/sadaqah) is central to Islam, embodying generosity, compassion, and social justice. AMCF strengthens this tradition by empowering donors, building collective giving platforms, and ensuring today’s generosity benefits future generations. It remains the only U.S. organization dedicated to Donor Advised Funds, Giving Circles, and Endowments for the Muslim community.

Vision

Leading sustainable & strategic Muslim philanthropy for today and future generations.

Mission

Cultivating donor giving and diversifying funding to advance charitable causes.
